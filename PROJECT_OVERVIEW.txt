## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

The **Multi-Camera Indian ANPR Traffic Intelligence Platform** is an enterprise-grade, edge-compatible surveillance and urban mobility analytics system specifically engineered for the high-variance environment of Indian roadways. Developed for the Smart India Hackathon (SIH) under Problem Statement ID 26127 for Bharat Electronics Limited (BEL), the platform tackles the severe real-world challenges of Indian traffic:
- Diverse vehicle classifications (two-wheelers, auto-rickshaws, commercial tempo vans, heavy trucks, buses, private cars).
- Varied license plate form factors (single-row horizontal plates, two-row stacked high-security plates, commercial yellow plates, EV green plates, rental black plates, and new Bharat Series 'BH' plates).
- Acute camera viewing angles, extreme lighting variance, headlight glare, vibration, and truncated frames where vehicle bodies are obscured.

The platform provides an end-to-end operational intelligence pipeline:
1. **Real-Time Edge Video Ingestion:** Multi-stream ingestion from RTSP IP surveillance cameras, local USB webcams, and mobile edge nodes paired via QR code WebRTC signaling.
2. **Direct Full-Frame Neural Plate Detection (ANPR V2):** Deep transformer (RF-DETR Small) and fine-tuned YOLOv8 ONNX models that localize license plates directly across full camera frames without vehicle-first gating dependencies.
3. **Sub-15ms Edge OCR Recognition:** Adaptive ensemble OCR (FastPlateOCR v2 + PP-OCRv4 ONNX fallback + fine-tuned CCT / CRNN architectures) executing in under 77 ms on CPU.
4. **Pure Indian Registration Validator (Zero Hallucination Policy):** Strict syntactic validation conforming to Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH) Rule 50, Bharat Series ('BH'), commercial, and vintage formats without artificial character morphing.
5. **Automated Journey Sessionization:** Intelligently splits raw multi-day vehicle sightings into distinct, physically plausible trips (default 30-minute inactivity threshold), eliminating multi-day false continuous trips and zero-speed artifacts.
6. **Road-Aware Routing & OSRM Engine Integration:** Turn-by-turn road centerline route mapping via OpenStreetMap/OSRM with camera-to-road snapping (<50m offset) and offline route caching, replacing unrealistic Euclidean straight lines.
7. **Predictive AI Route & Destination Forecasting:** Contextual Markov chain transition modeling that predicts next-camera arrival probabilities (85.57% Top-1, 100% Top-3, 0.928 MRR), multi-hop corridor projections, and dynamic congestion-aware ETAs.
8. **Camera Network Lifecycle & Failover Management:** Real-time state machine (`ONLINE`, `ACTIVE`, `DEGRADED`, `DISCONNECTED`, `OFFLINE`) with sub-millisecond connection/disconnection latency tracking and heartbeat health monitoring.
9. **Macro Urban Traffic Analytics:** Network density scoring, flow rates, Origin-Destination (OD) travel matrices, and congestion corridors mathematically verified (100% match) against controlled ground-truth datasets.
10. **Interactive GIS Spatial Intelligence & BOLO Operations:** Leaflet.js operator command center featuring density heatmaps, width-scaled route flows, vehicle trajectory playback with session filters, and Be-On-The-Lookout (BOLO) priority siren alerting with 60-second duplicate suppression.

## 2. Core Capabilities & Architectural Highlights

1. **Multi-Camera WebRTC & Mobile Edge Ingestion:**
   - Ingests high-definition live feeds concurrently from standard IP surveillance cameras, RTSP streams, USB webcams, and mobile smartphones acting as agile edge nodes.
   - Dynamic QR code generation enables instant on-field pairing of mobile phone cameras via secure HTTPS/WebRTC signaling.

2. **Direct Full-Frame Neural Plate Localization (ANPR V2):**
   - Directly detects license plates across the complete camera frame using RF-DETR Small with custom-trained YOLOv8 ONNX fallback.
   - Eliminates legacy vehicle-first prerequisite gating failures, successfully detecting plates on extreme close-ups, bumper crops, or when vehicle bodies are cropped out of frame.

3. **Adaptive Ensemble Edge OCR Recognition:**
   - Employs FastPlateOCR (Real-Data v2) combined with PP-OCRv4 ONNX fallback and specialized CRNN / CCT (Compact Convolutional Transformer) models.
   - Delivers 71.00% exact full-plate match across all held-out validation samples (81.78% on high-res plates, 98.89% on two-line plates) with ~76.8 ms CPU OCR inference latency.

4. **Non-Destructive Crop Rectification:**
   - Applies 4-point homography perspective unskewing for angled plates up to 45° with 10% expanded border padding, preventing the edge-character clipping common in older pipelines.

5. **Multi-Frame Temporal Consensus & Laplacian Sharpness Scoring:**
   - Tracks plate tracklets across continuous frames using Laplacian sharpness scoring (>60.0), selecting the clearest candidate frames.
   - Aggregates multi-frame sightings with character-level confidence-weighted voting to construct the final verified plate string.

6. **Pure Indian Registration Validator (Zero Hallucination Policy):**
   - Enforces strict parsing according to Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH) Rule 50 across all 36 States/UTs, Bharat Series ('BH'), commercial, and vintage formats.
   - Functions strictly as a **VALIDATOR** (returning `VALID`, `INVALID`, or `UNCERTAIN`). Completely eliminates legacy character-morphing heuristics that previously fabricated false registrations (e.g., swapping '8' with 'B' or '0' with 'O').

7. **Automated Journey Sessionization & Trip Segmentation:**
   - Solves the multi-day trip aggregation defect by splitting raw vehicle sightings into discrete journeys based on configurable inactivity thresholds (default: 30 minutes).
   - Computes realistic trip metrics (dwell time, road distance, segment speeds) and isolates active journeys from historical trips.

8. **Road-Aware Routing & OSRM Engine Integration:**
   - Snaps camera sensors installed on gantries/poles to the nearest routable road centerline (<50m snap distance).
   - Generates true road geometry using OSRM / OpenStreetMap routing engines, replacing Euclidean straight lines that sliced through buildings, rivers, and terrain.
   - Pre-caches road geometries in `data/camera_network.json` for 0ms sub-millisecond offline response without external API dependencies.

9. **Predictive AI Route & Destination Forecasting:**
   - Evaluates historical transition probabilities using first-order and second-order Markov chains, time-of-day weighting, and topological reachability.
   - Predicts the vehicle's most likely next camera node (85.57% Top-1, 100% Top-3 accuracy, 0.928 MRR), ranks candidate routes, and forecasts forward arrival ETAs based on live segment speeds.

10. **Camera Network Lifecycle & Failure Management:**
    - Real-time operational state machine enforcing `ONLINE`, `ACTIVE`, `DEGRADED`, `DISCONNECTED`, and `OFFLINE` status.
    - Monitors periodic camera heartbeats and tracks sub-millisecond transition latencies (0.008 ms disconnect, 0.013 ms reconnect) to detect camera stalling or hardware degradation immediately.

11. **Multi-Camera Bounded Concurrency Queue Management:**
    - `MultiCameraQueueManager` provides semaphore-bounded worker concurrency (`maxConcurrency: 2` on CPU) with depth-1 latest-frame queues per camera node.
    - Automatically drops stale backlogged frames to guarantee real-time, low-latency live video surveillance.

12. **Mathematically Validated Macro Traffic Flow Analytics:**
    - Modular analytics suite computing vehicle density tiers (LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH, SEVERE), camera-to-camera route flow volumes, speed distributions, Origin-Destination (OD) travel matrices, deterministic congestion scores, and time-bucketed flow trends.
    - 100% mathematically validated against dedicated controlled ground-truth datasets without data leakage.

13. **Interactive GIS Spatial Intelligence (Leaflet & Heatmaps):**
    - Leaflet.js engine featuring interactive camera nodes, normalized density heatmaps (`leaflet-heat.js`), width-scaled route density polylines, congestion warning corridors, solid cyan road-following trajectories, and green dashed predicted route corridors.

14. **Real-Time Watchlist & BOLO Security Alerting:**
    - Continuously matches incoming plate events against a Be-On-The-Lookout (BOLO) hotlist with tiered priority (CRITICAL, HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW), immediately firing audio HUD sirens and visual notifications across connected dashboards.
    - Implements a 60-second duplicate alert suppression cooldown per camera while ensuring immediate re-triggering upon arrival at a new camera node.

## 4. Modular Service Breakdown

### A. Core Vision Engine (`anpr_v2/` & `scripts/anpr_server.py`)
- **`anpr_v2/detector.py`**: Direct full-frame detector supporting RF-DETR Small transformer weights and YOLOv8 ONNX/PyTorch baseline adapter. Bypasses vehicle-level bounding box dependencies.
- **`anpr_v2/rectifier.py`**: Computes 4-point contour homography to deskew plates rotated up to 45°. Expands bounding boxes by 10% padding to prevent clipping of edge letters ('M', 'W', 'D', 'K').
- **`anpr_v2/recognizer.py`**: Implements Adaptive Ensemble routing, orchestrating FastPlateOCR (Real-Data v2) with PP-OCRv4 ONNX fallback, CRNN, and CCT models. Natively recognizes single-row and two-row stacked plates.
- **`anpr_v2/tracker.py`**: Multi-frame spatial tracklet manager. Computes Laplacian variance sharpness, maintains candidate pools, applies character-by-character confidence voting, and supports instant single-frame confirmation for manual diagnostic scans.
- **`anpr_v2/validator.py`**: Pure Indian registration validator strictly enforcing MoRTH syntax across all 36 States/UTs, Bharat Series (`YY BH NNNN AA`), commercial, and vintage formats.
- **`anpr_v2/pipeline.py`**: Orchestrates detection, rectification, recognition, tracking, and validation into a unified pipeline. Includes direct plate crop fallback for close-up photos.
- **`anpr_v2/config.py`**: Centralized configuration management for model paths, thresholds, and runtime execution flags.

### B. Streaming, Routing & Intelligence Microservices (`services/` & `server.js`)
- **`services/roadRoutingService.js`**: Road network routing engine implementing camera-to-road snapping (<50m offset), OSRM / OpenStreetMap route retrieval, multi-camera waypoint stitching, and persistent memory caching for 0ms offline route retrieval.
- **`services/routePredictionService.js`**: Predictive AI engine computing first-order and second-order Markov transition probabilities, direction alignment, U-turn reversal penalties, multi-hop corridor projections, next-camera candidate rankings, and congestion-adjusted ETA predictions.
- **`services/trajectoryService.js`**: Core journey reconstruction engine with automated sessionization (`segmentSightingsIntoJourneys`). Splits multi-day vehicle sightings into distinct trips based on inactivity thresholds (30 min), calculates true road distance (via `roadRoutingService`), travel times, leg speeds, and flags physical travel anomalies (>180 km/h).
- **`services/geoService.js`**: Implements the Haversine great-circle distance algorithm, segment travel time calculations, and speed estimation in km/h with strict input validation.
- **`services/trafficAnalytics.js`**: Calculates per-camera vehicle volumes, unique plate tallies, flow rates (veh/min, veh/5min, veh/h), and classifies real-time density levels (`LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`, `SEVERE`) across configurable time windows (15m, 30m, 1h, today).
- **`services/routeAnalytics.js`**: Computes transition counts between camera pairs, identifies top traffic corridors, and computes route-specific transit metrics.
- **`services/speedAnalytics.js`**: Measures network-wide weighted average speeds, route-specific speeds, and travel time variance using road distances.
- **`services/odAnalytics.js`**: Reconstructs complete Origin-Destination (OD) travel matrices across true network entry and exit camera nodes without intermediate node contamination.
- **`services/congestionAnalytics.js`**: Detects traffic congestion and bottleneck hotspots by evaluating current speeds and volumes against baseline parameters.
- **`services/trendAnalytics.js`**: Aggregates time-series traffic flow and average speeds into uniform time buckets (5m, 15m, 30m, 1h).
- **`services/analyticsService.js`**: Unified facade delivering executive dashboard summaries and scheduled Socket.io broadcast updates.
- **`services/analyticsUtils.js`**: Shared utilities for timestamp filtering, camera ID normalization, and 12-second burst deduplication.

### C. Frontend GIS Engine & Visualizations (`public/js/` & `public/dashboard.html`)
- **`public/js/trafficMap.js`**: Full-featured Leaflet.js GIS manager supporting 6 dynamic overlay layers:
  1. *Traffic Heatmap Layer*: Real-time density heatmap via `leaflet-heat.js` with auto-normalized intensity.
  2. *Route Density Layer*: Volume-weighted, color-coded polylines showing flow between camera nodes.
  3. *Congestion Corridors Layer*: Color-coded warning corridors highlighting traffic bottlenecks.
  4. *Road-Aligned Vehicle Trajectory Layer*: Turn-by-turn road centerline route visualization (`#00b4ff`) with waypoint popups, speeds, and timestamps.
  5. *Predicted Forward Route Layer*: Dashed green forward projection corridor (`#00ff41`) showing the vehicle's anticipated path to the next camera node.
  6. *Alternative Candidate Routes Layer*: Dashed amber paths (`#ffb400`) indicating secondary transition paths.
- **`public/js/anpr-engine.js`**: Client-side vision pipeline managing WebRTC video capture, Laplacian sharpness keyframe selection (>60.0), and HTML5 Canvas developer HUD overlays.
- **`public/dashboard.html`**: Zero-reload Single-Page Application (SPA) with 5 persistent tabs linked by hash routing (`#cmd`, `#track`, `#analytics`, `#network`, `#watchlist`).
- **`public/tracking.html`**: Dedicated standalone tracking view with vehicle journey session dropdown, road-aware route rendering, prediction probability badges, and dynamic ETA estimation.

## 9. Startup & Execution Instructions

### A. One-Click Cross-Platform Unified Launcher (Recommended)
The unified Python launcher executes system pre-flight checks, verifies port availability, launches both the Python ANPR microservice and Node.js server concurrently with live log streaming, and automatically opens the operator dashboard.

```bash
python run.py
```

#### Launcher Arguments & Options:
- `python run.py --no-browser` : Start all services in headless mode without launching browser.
- `python run.py --kill-existing` : Automatically kill and reclaim occupied ports (3000 and 5001).
- `python run.py --separate-windows` : Launch backend and frontend in separate console windows (Windows).
- `python run.py --backend-only` : Start only the Python ANPR V2 inference microservice (Port 5001).
- `python run.py --frontend-only` : Start only the Node.js streaming & analytics server (Port 3000).

### B. Native Windows Scripts
- **Command Prompt:** `start_system.bat`
- **PowerShell:** `powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File start_system.ps1`

### C. Manual Terminal Startup
- **Terminal 1 (Python Microservice):**
  ```bash
  python scripts/anpr_server.py
  ```
- **Terminal 2 (Node.js Backend):**
  ```bash
  node server.js
  ```

Once started, access the Operator Command Center at:
`https://127.0.0.1:3000/dashboard.html`

## 11. Production Roadmap & Future Enhancements

While the core AI recognition pipeline, road-aware routing, journey sessionization, predictive forecasting, and traffic analytics have been thoroughly modernized and benchmarked, the following enhancements remain on the long-term production deployment roadmap:

1. **Multi-Stream GPU Acceleration for 10+ Cameras:**
   - Current single-stream CPU inference executes in ~77 ms for OCR and ~82 ms for detection, easily supporting 2–4 streams concurrently.
   - For scaling to 10+ concurrent HD 30 FPS RTSP streams, deploy NVIDIA TensorRT execution provider or DirectML NPU acceleration.

2. **Optical Infrared (IR) Hardware Filtering for Night Glare:**
   - Oncoming high-beam headlights cause CCD sensor blooming at night, washing out plates located between lamps.
   - Deploy physical 850nm/940nm IR bandpass lens filters synchronized with pulsed infrared strobe illuminators.

3. **Appearance-Based Vehicle Re-Identification (Re-ID):**
   - Extract 512-dimensional chassis appearance embeddings (color, body type, modifications, roof racks, stickers) using an OSNet/ResNet model.
   - Enables continuous tracking of vehicles across cameras even when plates are physically removed, covered, or obscured by mud.

4. **Hierarchical Graph Neural Network (GNN) Routing:**
   - Upgrade from second-order Markov transitions to spatio-temporal Graph Neural Networks (e.g., ST-GCN) when scaling to 100+ camera nodes across city-scale traffic networks.
