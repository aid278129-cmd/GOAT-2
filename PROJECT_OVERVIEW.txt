## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

The **Multi-Camera Indian ANPR Traffic Intelligence Platform** is an enterprise-grade, edge-compatible surveillance and urban mobility analytics system specifically engineered for the high-variance environment of Indian roadways. Developed for the Smart India Hackathon (SIH) under Problem Statement ID 26127 for Bharat Electronics Limited (BEL), the platform tackles the severe real-world challenges of Indian traffic:
- Diverse vehicle classifications (two-wheelers, auto-rickshaws, commercial tempo vans, heavy trucks, buses, private cars).
- Varied license plate form factors (single-row horizontal plates, two-row stacked high-security plates, commercial yellow plates, EV green plates, rental black plates, and new Bharat Series 'BH' plates).
- Acute camera viewing angles, extreme lighting variance, headlight glare, vibration, and truncated frames where vehicle bodies are obscured.

The platform provides end-to-end capabilities from **real-time WebRTC multi-camera ingestion** and **direct full-frame neural license plate detection** to **sub-15ms edge OCR recognition**, **strict syntactic MoRTH validation**, **spatio-temporal vehicle trajectory tracking**, and **macro-level urban traffic intelligence** (density scoring, route flows, Origin-Destination matrices, congestion detection, and GIS heatmaps).

## 2. Core Capabilities & Architectural Highlights

1. **Multi-Camera WebRTC & Mobile Node Ingestion:**
   - Ingests high-definition live feeds concurrently from standard IP cameras, RTSP streams, USB webcams, and mobile smartphones acting as agile edge nodes.
   - Dynamic QR code generation enables instant on-field pairing of mobile phone cameras via secure HTTPS/WebRTC signaling.

2. **Direct Full-Frame Neural Plate Localization (ANPR V2):**
   - Directly detects license plates across the complete camera frame using RF-DETR Small with custom-trained YOLOv8 ONNX fallback.
   - Eliminates legacy vehicle-first prerequisite gating failures, successfully detecting plates even on extreme close-ups, bumper crops, or when vehicle bodies are cropped out of frame.

3. **Adaptive Ensemble Edge OCR Recognition:**
   - Employs FastPlateOCR (Real-Data v2) combined with PaddleOCR (PP-OCRv4) as a fallback via an Adaptive Ensemble routing mechanism.
   - Achieves 48.68% full-plate exact match rate on held-out datasets with processing latency around ~76.5 ms, completely avoiding dedicated high-power NVIDIA GPUs.

4. **Non-Destructive Crop Rectification:**
   - Applies 4-point homography perspective unskewing for angled plates up to 45° with 10% expanded border padding, preventing the edge-character clipping common in older pipelines.

5. **Multi-Frame Temporal Consensus & Sharpness Scoring:**
   - Tracks plate tracklets across continuous frames using Laplacian sharpness scoring, selecting the clearest candidate frames.
   - Aggregates multi-frame sightings with character-level confidence-weighted voting to construct the final verified plate string.

6. **Pure Indian Registration Validator (Zero Hallucination Policy):**
   - Enforces strict parsing according to Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH) Rule 50, Bharat Series ('BH'), commercial, and vintage formats.
   - Functions strictly as a **VALIDATOR** (returning `VALID`, `INVALID`, or `UNCERTAIN`). Completely eliminates legacy character-morphing heuristics that previously fabricated false registrations (e.g., swapping '8' with 'B' or '0' with 'O').

7. **Multi-Camera Bounded Concurrency Queue Management:**
   - `MultiCameraQueueManager` provides semaphore-bounded worker concurrency (`maxConcurrency: 2` on CPU) with depth-1 latest-frame queues per camera node.
   - Automatically drops stale backlogged frames to guarantee real-time, low-latency live video surveillance.

8. **Spatio-Temporal Vehicle Trajectory Reconstruction:**
   - Chronologically links individual vehicle detections across geographically distributed camera nodes.
   - Calculates leg distances (Haversine formula), transit durations, and leg-by-leg speeds (km/h) while automatically flagging physical impossibilities (>180 km/h) and duplicate camera bursts.

9. **Macro Traffic Flow & Movement Analytics:**
   - Modular analytics suite computing vehicle density tiers (LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH, SEVERE), camera-to-camera route flow volumes, speed distributions, Origin-Destination (OD) travel matrices, deterministic congestion scores, and time-bucketed flow trends.

10. **Interactive GIS Spatial Intelligence (Leaflet & Heatmaps):**
    - Leaflet.js engine featuring interactive camera nodes, normalized density heatmaps (`leaflet-heat.js`), width-scaled route density polylines, congestion warning corridors, and vehicle trajectory paths with animated playback.

11. **Real-Time Watchlist & BOLO Security Alerting:**
    - Continuously matches incoming plate events against a Be-On-The-Lookout (BOLO) hotlist with tiered priority (CRITICAL, HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW), immediately firing audio HUD sirens and visual notifications across connected dashboards.

## 4. Modular Service Breakdown

### A. Core Vision Engine (`anpr_v2/` & `scripts/anpr_server.py`)
- **`anpr_v2/detector.py`**: Direct full-frame detector supporting RF-DETR Small transformer weights and YOLOv8 ONNX/PyTorch baseline adapter. Bypasses vehicle-level bounding box dependencies.
- **`anpr_v2/rectifier.py`**: Computes 4-point contour homography to deskew plates rotated up to 45°. Expands bounding boxes by 10% padding to prevent clipping of edge letters ('M', 'W', 'D', 'K').
- **`anpr_v2/recognizer.py`**: Implements Adaptive Ensemble routing, orchestrating FastPlateOCR (Real-Data v2) with PP-OCRv4 fallback. Natively recognizes single-row and two-row stacked plates.
- **`anpr_v2/tracker.py`**: Multi-frame spatial tracklet manager. Computes Laplacian variance sharpness, maintains candidate pools, applies character-by-character confidence voting, and supports instant single-frame confirmation for manual diagnostic scans.
- **`anpr_v2/validator.py`**: Pure Indian registration validator strictly enforcing MoRTH syntax across all 36 States/UTs, Bharat Series (`YY BH NNNN AA`), commercial, and vintage formats.
- **`anpr_v2/pipeline.py`**: Orchestrates detection, rectification, recognition, tracking, and validation into a unified pipeline. Includes direct plate crop fallback for close-up photos.
- **`anpr_v2/config.py`**: Centralized configuration management for model paths, thresholds, and runtime execution flags.

### B. Streaming & Traffic Intelligence Microservices (`services/` & `server.js`)
- **`services/geoService.js`**: Implements the Haversine great-circle distance algorithm, segment travel time calculations, and speed estimation in km/h with strict input validation.
- **`services/trajectoryService.js`**: Chronologically stitches multi-camera sightings into end-to-end vehicle journeys. Computes leg transit durations, distances, leg speeds, and overall journey statistics while flagging impossible travel anomalies (>180 km/h) and deduplicating rapid single-camera bursts.
- **`services/trafficAnalytics.js`**: Calculates per-camera vehicle volumes, unique plate tallies, and classifies real-time density levels (`LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`, `SEVERE`) across configurable time windows (15m, 30m, 1h, today).
- **`services/routeAnalytics.js`**: Computes transition counts between camera pairs, identifies top traffic corridors, and computes route-specific transit metrics.
- **`services/speedAnalytics.js`**: Measures network-wide weighted average speeds, route-specific speeds, and travel time variance.
- **`services/odAnalytics.js`**: Reconstructs complete Origin-Destination (OD) travel matrices across network entry and exit camera nodes.
- **`services/congestionAnalytics.js`**: Detects traffic congestion and bottleneck hotspots by evaluating current speeds and volumes against baseline parameters.
- **`services/trendAnalytics.js`**: Aggregates time-series traffic flow and average speeds into uniform time buckets (5m, 15m, 30m, 1h).
- **`services/analyticsService.js`**: Unified facade delivering executive dashboard summaries and scheduled Socket.io broadcast updates.
- **`services/analyticsUtils.js`**: Shared utilities for timestamp filtering, camera ID normalization, and 12-second burst deduplication.

### C. Frontend GIS Engine & Visualizations (`public/js/` & `public/dashboard.html`)
- **`public/js/trafficMap.js`**: Full-featured Leaflet.js GIS manager supporting 4 dynamic overlay layers:
  1. *Traffic Heatmap Layer*: Real-time density heatmap via `leaflet-heat.js` with auto-normalized intensity.
  2. *Route Density Layer*: Volume-weighted, color-coded polylines showing flow between camera nodes.
  3. *Congestion Corridors Layer*: Color-coded warning corridors highlighting traffic bottlenecks.
  4. *Vehicle Trajectory Layer*: Step-by-step route visualization with waypoint popups, speeds, and timestamps.
- **`public/js/anpr-engine.js`**: Client-side vision pipeline managing WebRTC video capture, Laplacian sharpness keyframe selection (>60.0), and HTML5 Canvas developer HUD overlays.
- **`public/dashboard.html`**: Zero-reload Single-Page Application (SPA) with 5 persistent tabs linked by hash routing (`#cmd`, `#track`, `#analytics`, `#network`, `#watchlist`).

## 9. Startup & Execution Instructions

### A. One-Click Cross-Platform Unified Launcher (Recommended)
The unified Python launcher executes system pre-flight checks, verifies port availability, launches both the Python ANPR microservice and Node.js server concurrently with live log streaming, and automatically opens the operator dashboard.

```bash
python run.py
```

#### Launcher Arguments & Options:
- `python run.py --no-browser` : Start all services in headless mode without launching browser.
- `python run.py --kill-existing` : Automatically kill and reclaim occupied ports (3000 and 5001).
- `python run.py --separate-windows` : Launch backend and frontend in separate console windows (Windows).
- `python run.py --backend-only` : Start only the Python ANPR V2 inference microservice (Port 5001).
- `python run.py --frontend-only` : Start only the Node.js streaming & analytics server (Port 3000).

### B. Native Windows Scripts
- **Command Prompt:** `start_system.bat`
- **PowerShell:** `powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File start_system.ps1`

### C. Manual Terminal Startup
- **Terminal 1 (Python Microservice):**
  ```bash
  python scripts/anpr_server.py
  ```
- **Terminal 2 (Node.js Backend):**
  ```bash
  node server.js
  ```

Once started, access the Operator Command Center at:
`https://127.0.0.1:3000/dashboard.html`

## 11. Production Roadmap & Future Enhancements

While the core AI recognition pipeline, trajectory tracking, and traffic analytics have been thoroughly modernized and benchmarked, the following enhancements remain on the long-term production deployment roadmap:

1. **Multi-Stream GPU Acceleration for 10+ Cameras (Medium Priority):**
   - Current single-stream CPU inference executes in ~11 ms for OCR and ~100 ms for detection, easily supporting 2–4 streams.
   - For scaling to 10+ concurrent HD 30 FPS RTSP streams, deploy NVIDIA TensorRT execution provider or DirectML NPU acceleration.

2. **Optical Infrared (IR) Hardware Filtering for Night Glare:**
   - Oncoming high-beam headlights cause CCD sensor blooming at night, washing out plates located between lamps.
   - Deploy physical 850nm/940nm IR bandpass lens filters synchronized with pulsed infrared strobe illuminators.

3. **Appearance-Based Vehicle Re-Identification (Re-ID):**
   - Extract 512-dimensional chassis appearance embeddings (color, body type, modifications, stickers) using an OSNet/ResNet model.
   - Enables continuous tracking of vehicles across cameras even when plates are physically removed, covered, or obscured by mud.
